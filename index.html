Passion Fruit Boba Tea

A sweet and tangy recipe you can make at home.

Hi, I'm Pearl!

Ingredients (makes 1 cup)

  • 2 fresh passion fruits
  • 1 black tea bag
  • 1/2 cup hot water
  • 2 tablespoons tapioca pearls
  • 1 tablespoon brown sugar
  • 1 tablespoon honey
  • Ice cubes

Steps

  1. Cook the tapioca pearls in boiling water for about 15 minutes, then rinse them.
  2. Soak the pearls in brown sugar for 10 minutes so they get sweet.
  3. Brew the tea bag in hot water for 3 minutes and let it cool.
  4. Scoop out the passion fruit pulp and mix it with the honey.
  5. Put the pearls in a glass, add ice, then pour in the passion fruit mix.
  6. Pour the tea on top, stir and enjoy!

Tips

Add more honey if your passion fruit is very sour. You can also swap the black tea for green tea.
